I am a digital collections specialist from and currently based in Brooklyn, New York.

I draw inspiration from ways of sensing and organizing the world that come to appear coherent — or strange — depending on where one stands. Using both computational and interpretive methods, my work explores how media systems, datasets, and museum collections translate unfamiliar cultural forms into legible knowledge. I am currently completing my master’s degree in Museums & Digital Culture @ Pratt Info School

I care for a Chihuahua/Dachshund mix named Thomas, and enjoy Ken Burns documentaries, reality television, and "stewarding" a collection vintage shopping finds.
